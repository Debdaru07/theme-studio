Different themes for a SaaS for different clients, 

* What's the benefit ?
- We can build a SaaS but for pitching to different clients which has their own themes and brands, we need a tightly coupled dynamic-themeing system. 

* How can they use ?
- A company enrolls on our subscription for handling dynamic theming for clients coming with pre-rich knowledge of color-palettes, definitions and defining its themes. 

Now when that company tries to pitch to its various clients, they need to make entries against each client for their brandings.
