Core Theming Pattern, 


* Brand Colors : primary, secondary, accent
* Neutral / Surface Colors : surface, surfaceDark
* Text Colors : onSurface, onSurfaceMuted, onSurfaceDark, onSurfaceDarkMuted
* Branding Text Colors : ,
* Branding Surface Colors : ,
* Semantics : success, error, warning.

Type of Color / Purpose / Used where ? 

* Brand Colors / Identity / Primary buttons, links, active states, selected tabs, progress
* Neutral -or- Surface / Background hierarchy / App bg, cards, sheets, dialogs, app bars
* Text Colors / Readability / Headings, body text, captions, disabled text
* Branding Text Colors / Text on brand Backgrounds / Text -or- icons on Primary, Secondary, Accent buttons or banners (onPrimary, onSecondary)

* Branding Surface Colors / Brand-tinted backgrounds / PrimaryContainer, SecondaryContainer, promotional cards, chips, badges
* Semantic Colors / Status & Feedback / Success, Error, Warning, Info, Loading, validation
