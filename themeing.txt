A SaaS Theme comprises of the following category of items : 

1. Colors - Brand, Surfaces, States, Text Colors, Semantics.

2. Typography - Font Family, sizes, weights, line-heights

3. Spacing - Margins, Paddings, gaps, layout grid.

4. Sizing - Icon Sizes, button heights, input heights, breakpoints.

5. Shapes - Border radius, corner styles, stroke widths

6. Elevation - Shadows, overlays, z-index, depth

7. Components - Buttons, Cards, Inputs, Dialogs, Chips, etc.

8. Motion - Durations, easing curves, animations.

9. Icons and Illustrations - Icon Set, style (outlined/filled), illustration language. 

10. Effects - Blur, Opacity, gradients, glassmorphism, focus rings.
